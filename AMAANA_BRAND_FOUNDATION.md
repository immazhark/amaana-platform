# Amaana Foundation — Brand Foundation

Status: LOCKED BRAND INPUT
Branch: `phase-public-site-rebuild`

## Brand name

The public brand name is **Amaana Foundation**.

Do not abbreviate the primary public identity to “Amaana” in the logo/brand lockup unless the design context clearly calls for a conversational shorthand. The formal public brand remains **Amaana Foundation**.

## Core brand colours

Official anchor colours supplied by the Foundation:

- Amaana Blue: `#466FAA`
- Amaana Gold: `#E0B318`

These colours are brand anchors, not a rigid two-colour restriction.

### Design rule

The website should unmistakably reflect the Amaana Foundation identity while allowing a sophisticated tonal system around the official colours. The official blue and gold may be used exactly where appropriate, and may also be supported by nearby tonal variants for dark surfaces, hover states, accessibility, editorial backgrounds, photography overlays, data visualisation, and subtle motion.

Examples of permitted supporting treatment:
- deeper navy/ink derived from the Amaana blue for cinematic dark sections
- softer/desaturated blue tints for backgrounds and secondary UI
- warmer or quieter gold variants for large surfaces where the exact `#E0B318` would be visually too intense
- warm ivory, parchment, sand, off-white, and neutral ink as supporting editorial colours

The goal is not to force `#466FAA` and `#E0B318` onto every component. The goal is that the complete visual system feels recognisably descended from those two official colours.

## Guardrails

1. Do not replace the Amaana identity with generic NGO green.
2. Do not turn the interface into a blue-and-yellow corporate template.
3. Use the exact logo artwork from the supplied brand archive once production assets are ingested.
4. Build contrast-safe tonal variants rather than altering brand colours arbitrarily.
5. Photography remains dominant; brand colour should frame and unify authentic Amaana media rather than overpower it.
6. Islamic identity should come through composition, values, typography, geometry, content and restraint—not colour clichés.
7. Final design tokens must be visually checked against the real logo and campaign materials before launch.

## Relationship to V2 concept

The **Living Amanah** direction remains unchanged. The updated visual language is:

**Amaana Blue (`#466FAA`) + Amaana Gold (`#E0B318`) + deep derived ink/navy + warm editorial neutrals + authentic photography.**

This brand foundation takes precedence over any earlier provisional palette suggestions in the V2 implementation plan.
