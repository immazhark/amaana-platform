## Brand name

The public brand name is **Amaana Foundation**.

Do not abbreviate the primary public identity to “Amaana” in the logo/brand lockup unless the design context clearly calls for conversational shorthand. The formal public brand remains **Amaana Foundation**.

## Current working brand colours

Amaana's supplied campaign/contact material consistently shows a blue-and-gold identity. The current working anchor values used during design are:

- Working Amaana Blue: `#466FAA`
- Working Amaana Gold: `#E0B318`

**Important certification boundary:** these values must not be described as master/official source values until the original files inside the supplied `Branding & Logo.zip` are successfully inspected. The archive exists and has been materialized, but the current runtime repeatedly times out while enumerating/extracting it.

The working values may continue to guide the rebuild because they are consistent with the supplied branded material, but final design tokens must be reconciled against the original logo/source artwork before launch.

## Design rule

The website should unmistakably reflect the Amaana Foundation identity while allowing a sophisticated tonal system around the blue-and-gold anchors. Once the master source values are verified, they may be supported by nearby tonal variants for dark surfaces, hover states, accessibility, editorial backgrounds, photography overlays, data visualisation and restrained motion.

Examples of permitted supporting treatment:
- deeper navy/ink derived from the Amaana blue for cinematic dark sections;
- softer/desaturated blue tints for backgrounds and secondary UI;
- warmer or quieter gold variants for large surfaces where the source gold would be visually too intense;
- warm ivory, parchment, sand, off-white and neutral ink as supporting editorial colours.

The goal is not to force blue and gold onto every component. The goal is for the complete visual system to feel recognisably descended from Amaana's actual identity.

## Guardrails

1. Do not replace the Amaana identity with generic NGO green.
2. Do not turn the interface into a blue-and-yellow corporate template.
3. Use the exact logo artwork from the supplied brand archive once the master asset is successfully recovered.
4. Do not crop, redraw, trace, AI-recreate or silently approximate a logo and call it the master.
5. Build contrast-safe tonal variants only after comparing them with the verified source artwork.
6. Photography remains dominant; brand colour should frame and unify authentic Amaana media rather than overpower it.
7. Islamic identity should come through composition, values, typography, geometry, content and restraint—not colour clichés.
8. Logo schema, final social-preview identity and production brand-token certification remain blocked until the original master asset is inspected.
