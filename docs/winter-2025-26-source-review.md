# Winter Drive 2025–26 source review

Bounded first publication batch reviewed ten of 30 supplied files on 14 September 2026: AWD1, AWD2, WKT1, WKT2, WKT3, WKT8, Amaana Winter Drive.png, the first two kit-preparation photographs, and one carpet-room photograph.

Selected: one non-identifying preparation photograph, WKT2 impact update, and the original campaign banner. WKT2 reports 234 winter kits, each including a sweater or jacket, blanket and beanie cap. AWD2 independently reports reaching 234 people and describes recipient groups. The page identifies these as campaign-reported figures.

Omitted from this page: generic covers/closing slide; donor-specific WKT3; near-duplicate preparation photo; carpet evidence, which is a separate activity; and one photograph showing identifiable children. No recipient photo was selected because publication consent has not been supplied.

All selected originals were downloaded from the inventoried Drive IDs, matched expected byte sizes, converted without cropping, and fully decoded. Import remains additive and preview-only. Twenty files in this folder remain unreviewed, so this is not a complete folder audit.

Direct-upload update: the user supplied seven local photographs and two local videos and instructed that Google Drive no longer be used. The two preparation photographs contain no people and are selected. Five distribution photographs show identifiable adults or children and remain unpublished pending a confirmed publication-consent basis. The videos remain unpublished because the browser security policy blocked local-file playback and no approved local codec was available; they were not guessed from filenames. The selected photos are sourced as `user-upload:` records rather than Drive links.
