# Dates Distribution 2023–2026 source review

Reviewed 14 September 2026 from files supplied directly by the user. Google Drive was not used.

## Reported editions

- 2026: 162 kg. One campaign update, nine photographs and one 13.8-second preparation video supplied.
- 2025: 90 kg. Four preparation/product photographs supplied.
- 2024: 90 kg. One 94.4-second distribution video supplied.
- 2023: 90 kg. One photograph and three distribution videos supplied. At the user's direction, only the shortest video (38.8 seconds) is selected; the two approximately 171-second clips are excluded.

The weights above are campaign-reported quantities supplied by the user. Recipient counts, expenditure and delivery locations are not inferred from visible boxes, packages or people.

## Privacy processing

The 2026 handover photographs, the 2023 photograph, and the selected 2023 and 2024 videos contain identifiable people. Public derivatives use strong face/head blurring. Video processing combines face detection with person detection to protect distant, turned and partly occluded faces across adjacent frames. Representative frames were visually reviewed after processing, and a second detector pass found no unblurred faces in the selected 2023 and 2024 video samples.

Original files remain unchanged outside the repository. Only optimized and privacy-protected derivatives are public. Public captions explicitly identify blurred material.

## Editorial boundaries

Each year is a separate initiative page under Seasonal & Food Support. Copy describes only what the supplied media and campaign figures support. The 2026 page is a source-guarded revision of its existing published record; 2025, 2024 and 2023 are new idempotent archive imports.
