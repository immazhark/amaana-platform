# Assistance communication templates (owner use)

Drafts for the owner to send manually. Nothing here is sent automatically. Replace `{FORM_URL}` with the verified assistance form address (`/request-assistance` on the official site). Do not ask for medical or identity documents in public comments or open chats.

## A. WhatsApp away / standard reply
Assalamu alaikum. Thank you for contacting Amaana Foundation. We are a small volunteer-led organisation. We currently consider individual assistance only for people living in our Hyderabad service area, and we can assess only a small number of cases of severe, verifiable hardship. We do not accept new assistance requests by phone call or WhatsApp. Please use our form: {FORM_URL}. Submitting a request does not guarantee assessment or support. If we need more information, our team will contact you. We are not an emergency service; please do not delay urgent care.

## B. Outside area
Thank you for writing to us, and we are sorry for your difficulty. Our current work is limited to beneficiaries in our Hyderabad service area, so we cannot assess or support this request. This is a limit of our small capacity, not a judgement of your need. We pray you find the help you need.

## C. Outside scope
Thank you for explaining your situation. This request falls outside the individual hardship priorities we can currently consider, so we are unable to take it forward. We wish you well.

## D. Capacity
Thank you for your request. We are not able to take up additional cases at present. This does not reflect on your need, and we cannot say whether or when a place may open. Please do not wait on us for urgent care.
