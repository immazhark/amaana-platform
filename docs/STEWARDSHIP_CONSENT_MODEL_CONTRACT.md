# Optional donor stewardship — unpublished synthetic consent model

AF-CRM engineering-only groundwork, not a donor CRM, mailing list or permission to contact anyone. The transactional acknowledgement queue and its delivery gates remain unchanged. Donation, receipt, sponsorship, payment status and donor email do not imply optional marketing or stewardship consent.

The model evaluates a synthetic opaque subject with two proposed purpose identifiers (impact-updates and monthly-giving), an explicit as-of timestamp and the exact reviewed template version. It accepts only a caller-supplied complete ordered history starting at sequence 1, with contiguous sequence numbers, nondecreasing timestamps and no future records. The latest explicit purpose grant or withdrawal determines that purpose alone. A global opt-out, hard bounce or complaint suppresses all optional purposes; the model deliberately cannot remove such a suppression. A later explicit grant may follow a topic-only withdrawal, but does not override global suppression. A reviewed template assertion must match both purpose and version. These are conservative prototype decisions, not an approved consent policy.

The output is a candidate flag and a fixed reason, never an email address, message, authorization token or provider action. Unknown, malformed, repeated, out-of-order, cross-subject and oversized inputs fail closed. Time O(n), auxiliary space O(1), maximum history 10,000 events. No network, database, clock, real donor data or side effects. The caller must provide a complete trusted snapshot: this model cannot prove event authenticity, identity ownership, completeness of a truncated tail, actual template approval, current provider suppression or concurrency safety. It is not a send-time gate.

Before runtime integration, separately review purpose wording, consent provenance, double opt-in and resubscription policy, private identity and rights/retention boundaries, verified provider suppression and webhook handling, reviewed immutable templates, atomic queue/send eligibility and revocation races, bounded audit and access rules. Do not attach this to the worker, introduce a schema, send a message, import a list, activate a cron or change payment/transactional email behaviour as part of this scope.
