# Amaana Foundation — Master Digital Experience Plan

Status: CANONICAL PROJECT PLAN
Branch: phase-public-site-rebuild

This document is the source of truth for the public Amaana Foundation website. Every public-site iteration, modification, review, and completion decision must be checked against it. If requirements change, update this document first or in the same change; do not silently drift away from it.

## 1. Product vision

Amaana Foundation must feel stunning, heartwarming, dignified, trustworthy, unmistakably Islamic in values and character, and alive with real evidence of service. Visitors should immediately understand who Amaana is, see authentic work, feel an emotional connection, trust the organization, and naturally continue exploring.

The website is not an admin application with public pages attached. It is Amaana's authoritative public story, impact archive, active appeal platform, social bridge, assistance gateway, and trust/transparency layer.

## 3. Design principles

1. Real Amaana media first. Use authentic photos/video whenever appropriate; do not substitute stock imagery for supplied Amaana material.
2. Emotional but dignified. Never exploit beneficiaries, manufacture urgency, or use pity-driven imagery/copy.
3. Islamic values are visible. Faith is part of Amaana's identity, not a hidden footnote.
4. Islamic content is purposeful and verified. Qur'anic verses, hadith, Arabic text, Zakat/Sadaqah terminology and religious claims must be accurately sourced and contextually appropriate before publication.
5. Editorial, not template-like. Pages should use varied layouts, immersive photography, storytelling, strong typography, meaningful whitespace and restrained visual motifs instead of endless identical rounded cards.
6. Motion supports attention. Use tasteful carousel transitions, image movement, scroll reveals, counter animation and hover feedback with reduced-motion support; no gratuitous SaaS/glassmorphism effects.
7. Mobile is first-class. No disappearing navigation, broken carousels or cramped donation flows.
8. Accessibility is mandatory. Semantic HTML, visible focus, keyboard access, sufficient contrast, alt text, labels and reduced-motion support.
9. Trust is demonstrated. Link claims to reports, initiative pages, photographs, updates, compliance disclosures and real outcomes.
10. No dead ends. Every empty state must lead to a useful next action without inventing donation categories or programmes.

## 4. Brand and visual direction

Build from Amaana's real logo, supplied campaign creatives and authentic photography. The intended feel is warm, premium, human and faith-grounded rather than generic NGO green-and-gold.

Potential visual language to validate against actual assets:
- warm cream / off-white foundations
- deep navy / ink for authority and depth
- restrained warm gold accents
- secondary colours derived from Amaana's real media/brand assets
- subtle Islamic geometric motifs, arches, crescents, lantern/mosque references only where suitable
- strong editorial serif/sans pairing or equivalent distinctive typography
- large authentic photography, edge-to-edge moments and layered image/text compositions

Do not finalize colours or typography until the real logo/assets are inventoried.

## 6. Content and asset ingestion rule

Every prior Amaana file must be reviewed, not merely stored.

For each PDF, DOC/DOCX, image, campaign creative, video or prior appeal asset record:
- source filename
- type
- date/year
- initiative/campaign
- text/data extracted
- image/video content summarized
- public/private/sensitive classification
- potential page(s)
- factual claims requiring verification
- caption/context
- use now / archive / duplicate / private / needs clarification

Instagram/post graphics are dual-purpose sources: the image itself may be useful, and the text/statistics embedded in the graphic may contain copy, dates, beneficiary counts, donor acknowledgements, Islamic references or programme facts that should be converted into structured web content after verification.

## 7. Social media requirements

The published site must explicitly surface Amaana's approved Instagram, YouTube and Facebook presence. The exact handles/URLs are to be verified against supplied assets/accounts before coding them as final.

Required placements:
- footer
- Contact
- Follow Our Work section on Home

- share links on appropriate public stories/appeals

## 8. Content voice

Amaana voice should be:
- compassionate
- humble
- faith-grounded
- dignified
- specific
- transparent
- local and human

Avoid generic AI charity language such as 'Give hope where it is needed most' unless it is genuinely part of Amaana's approved voice. Prefer concrete stories, real actions, real dates, real numbers and real words from Amaana's own material.

## 9. Retention and engagement strategy

Visitors stay because there is meaningful content to discover, not because effects trap them.

Use:
- compelling hero carousel
- visual programme gateways
- editorial story modules
- photo/video galleries and lightboxes
- related-content pathways
- real-time/recent activity where maintainable
- initiative timelines
- impact exploration
- social follow-through
- updates/news
- subtle motion
- clear contextual CTAs

Avoid:
- autoplay audio
- intrusive popups
- manipulative countdowns
- fake urgency
- fake donor avatars
- excessive animation
- unverified live counters

## 10. Core workflows to design and QA

1. First-time visitor -> understands Amaana -> explores work -> sees evidence -> follows/social or takes action.
2. Donor -> active appeal/support option -> details/evidence -> donation -> payment confirmation -> receipt/acknowledgement -> updates.
3. No-active-appeal donor -> useful explanation -> completed impact/history -> legitimate available support action or follow/social; never a blank dead end.
4. Assistance requester -> understands process/privacy -> submits request/docs -> receives reference -> staff can review -> requester can receive updates through approved channel.
5. Returning supporter -> sees recent activity/new stories/social content -> explores latest work -> acts again.
6. Trust-seeking visitor -> About/Team -> verification/process -> compliance/reports -> contact.
7. Mobile visitor -> full navigation, galleries, forms and donation flow work without lost functionality.

## 11. Technical release blockers

Must be resolved before launch:
- staging/non-production noindex
- production indexing enabled only deliberately
- mobile navigation
- payment-state correctness and idempotency edge cases
- assistance-request abuse/rate limiting
- staff notification behavior
- private object-storage deployment/retention/access validation
- secure handling of uploaded documents
- consent/privacy for public beneficiary media and case data
- accessibility
- performance/image optimization
- error and empty states

## 12. Acceptance gates

A page or feature is NOT complete because it builds, returns 200, or looks acceptable in one screenshot.

Every public page must pass:
- content-source check against Amaana files/data
- benchmark check against relevant reference patterns in this document
- Amaana identity check
- authentic-media check
- Islamic-content check where relevant
- social-integration check where relevant
- desktop visual QA
- mobile visual QA
- keyboard/accessibility QA
- responsive/performance QA
- CTA/workflow QA
- empty/loading/error-state QA
- privacy/compliance review
- independent review

## 13. Iteration governance — anti-drift rule

Before starting an iteration:
1. Read this document.
2. Identify which product goals and benchmark patterns apply.
3. Check the content/asset register for real Amaana source material.
4. State any proposed deviation from this plan.

During implementation:
5. Preserve working backend contracts unless a verified defect requires change.
6. Do not replace real content with placeholders for convenience.
7. Do not invent programmes, religious claims, donation categories, statistics or compliance language.

Before calling the iteration complete:
8. Re-read the relevant sections of this document.
9. Run the acceptance gates.
10. Compare the result to at least the most relevant benchmark sites, not to the old Amaana frontend.
11. Record unresolved gaps.
12. If the product direction changed, update this document so the next iteration inherits the decision.

## 15. Final launch standard

A visitor who knows nothing about Amaana should be able to answer, after exploring the site:
- Who is Amaana Foundation?
- Why does it exist?
- What Islamic values guide it?
- Who are the people behind it?
- What has it actually done?
- What proof can I see?
- What is happening now?
- How are requests/cases handled?
- How can I donate or otherwise help?
- How can I request assistance?
- Where can I follow ongoing activity?
- What is Amaana's legal/compliance position?

If the website cannot answer those questions beautifully, clearly and credibly, it is not finished.
